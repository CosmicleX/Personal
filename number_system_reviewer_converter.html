Quick Conversion Shortcuts

Binary ↔ Hexadecimal

Group binary bits into sets of 4 (starting from right). Each group equals one Hex digit (8-4-2-1 rule).

Binary ↔ Octal

Group binary bits into sets of 3 (starting from right). Each group equals one Octal digit (4-2-1 rule).

Hexadecimal ↔ Octal

Never go through Decimal! Convert to Binary first as a bridge, regroup the bits (by 3s or 4s), and convert to the target base.

Comprehensive Conversion Guide & Examples

1. Decimal to Other Bases (Successive Division)

Decimal to Binary (Base 10 → Base 2)

Divide the decimal number by 2. Record the remainder. Continue dividing the quotient by 2 until it reaches 0. Read remainders from bottom to top.

Example 1: Convert 45₁₀
45 ÷ 2 = 22   R 1   ↑ (read up)
22 ÷ 2 = 11   R 0   |
11 ÷ 2 = 5    R 1   |
5  ÷ 2 = 2    R 1   |
2  ÷ 2 = 1    R 0   |
1  ÷ 2 = 0    R 1   |
Result: 101101₂
Example 2: Convert 156₁₀
156 ÷ 2 = 78   R 0   ↑
78  ÷ 2 = 39   R 0   |
39  ÷ 2 = 19   R 1   |
19  ÷ 2 = 9    R 1   |
9   ÷ 2 = 4    R 1   |
4   ÷ 2 = 2    R 0   |
2   ÷ 2 = 1    R 0   |
1   ÷ 2 = 0    R 1   |
Result: 10011100₂

Decimal to Octal (Base 10 → Base 8)

Divide by 8. Record the remainder. Read remainders bottom to top.

Example 1: Convert 156₁₀
156 ÷ 8 = 19   R 4   ↑
19  ÷ 8 = 2    R 3   |
2   ÷ 8 = 0    R 2   |
Result: 234₈
Example 2: Convert 400₁₀
400 ÷ 8 = 50   R 0   ↑
50  ÷ 8 = 6    R 2   |
6   ÷ 8 = 0    R 6   |
Result: 620₈

Decimal to Hexadecimal (Base 10 → Base 16)

Divide by 16. Record the remainder (Convert 10-15 to A-F). Read bottom to top.

Example 1: Convert 254₁₀
254 ÷ 16 = 15   R 14 (E) ↑
15  ÷ 16 = 0    R 15 (F) |
Result: FE₁₆
Example 2: Convert 419₁₀
419 ÷ 16 = 26   R 3       ↑
26  ÷ 16 = 1    R 10 (A) |
1   ÷ 16 = 0    R 1       |
Result: 1A3₁₆
2. Other Bases to Decimal (Positional Expansion)

Binary to Decimal (Base 2 → Base 10)

Multiply each bit by 2 raised to its position index (starting from 0 on the far right), or simply add up the powers of 2 (128, 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1) where a '1' exists.

Example 1: Convert 10110₂
Bits:    1   0   1   1   0
Weights: 16   8   4   2   1
Math:    16 + 0 + 4 + 2 + 0
Result: 22₁₀
Example 2: Convert 1101101₂
Bits:    1   1   0   1   1   0   1
Weights: 64  32  16   8   4   2   1
Math:    64 +32 + 0 + 8 + 4 + 0 + 1
Result: 109₁₀

Octal to Decimal (Base 8 → Base 10)

Multiply each digit by 8 raised to its position (..., 64, 8, 1).

Example 1: Convert 45₈
Math: (4 × 8¹) + (5 × 8⁰)
Math: (4 × 8) + (5 × 1)
Math: 32 + 5
Result: 37₁₀
Example 2: Convert 317₈
Math: (3 × 8²) + (1 × 8¹) + (7 × 8⁰)
Math: (3 × 64) + (1 × 8) + (7 × 1)
Math: 192 + 8 + 7
Result: 207₁₀

Hexadecimal to Decimal (Base 16 → Base 10)

Multiply each digit by 16 raised to its position (..., 256, 16, 1). Remember A=10, B=11, C=12, D=13, E=14, F=15.

Example 1: Convert 2A₁₆
Math: (2 × 16¹) + (A × 16⁰)
Math: (2 × 16) + (10 × 1)
Math: 32 + 10
Result: 42₁₀
Example 2: Convert 1F4₁₆
Math: (1 × 16²) + (F × 16¹) + (4 × 16⁰)
Math: (1 × 256) + (15 × 16) + (4 × 1)
Math: 256 + 240 + 4
Result: 500₁₀
3. Binary ↔ Octal (The 3-Bit Grouping Method)

Binary to Octal (Base 2 → Base 8)

Group the bits into sets of 3, starting from the right. Pad with leading zeros if needed. Convert each 3-bit group to its Octal value (using 4-2-1 rule).

Example 1: Convert 110101₂
1. Group by 3:   (110) (101)
2. Apply 4-2-1:    6     5
Result: 65₈
Example 2: Convert 1011100₂
1. Pad w/ zeros: (001) (011) (100)
2. Apply 4-2-1:    1     3     4
Result: 134₈

Octal to Binary (Base 8 → Base 2)

Convert each single Octal digit into exactly 3 binary bits.

Example 1: Convert 72₈
1. Split digits:     7      2
2. To 3-bit Binary: (111) (010)
Result: 111010₂
Example 2: Convert 305₈
1. Split digits:     3      0      5
2. To 3-bit Binary: (011) (000) (101)
Result: 011000101₂ (or 11000101₂)
4. Binary ↔ Hexadecimal (The 4-Bit Grouping Method)

Binary to Hexadecimal (Base 2 → Base 16)

Group bits by 4 from the right. Apply the 8-4-2-1 rule to get values from 0-15 (Convert 10-15 to A-F).

Example 1: Convert 11010110₂
1. Group by 4:  (1101)  (0110)
2. Apply 8-4-2-1: 13(D)   6
Result: D6₁₆
Example 2: Convert 1011011₂
1. Pad w/ zeros:  (0101)  (1011)
2. Apply 8-4-2-1:  5      11(B)
Result: 5B₁₆

Hexadecimal to Binary (Base 16 → Base 2)

Convert each single Hex digit into exactly 4 binary bits.

Example 1: Convert C4₁₆
1. Split digits:   C (12)    4
2. To 4-bit Binary: (1100)   (0100)
Result: 11000100₂
Example 2: Convert 2F9₁₆
1. Split digits:   2       F(15)   9
2. To 4-bit Binary: (0010)  (1111)  (1001)
Result: 001011111001₂ (or 1011111001₂)
5. Octal ↔ Hexadecimal (The Binary Bridge)

Octal to Hexadecimal (Base 8 → Base 16)

Step 1: Convert Octal to Binary (3 bits per digit). Step 2: Regroup the Binary bits into 4s. Step 3: Convert 4-bit groups to Hex.

Example 1: Convert 56₈
1. Octal to Bin: 5(101) 6(110) → 101110₂
2. Regroup by 4: (0010) (1110)
3. Bin to Hex:    2      E(14)
Result: 2E₁₆
Example 2: Convert 374₈
1. Oct to Bin: 3(011) 7(111) 4(100)
2. Regroup by 4: (0000) (1111) (1100)
3. Bin to Hex:    0     F(15)  C(12)
Result: FC₁₆

Hexadecimal to Octal (Base 16 → Base 8)

Step 1: Convert Hex to Binary (4 bits per digit). Step 2: Regroup the Binary bits into 3s. Step 3: Convert 3-bit groups to Octal.

Example 1: Convert A7₁₆
1. Hex to Bin: A(1010) 7(0111)
2. Regroup by 3: (010) (100) (111)
3. Bin to Octal:  2     4     7
Result: 247₈
Example 2: Convert 1DB₁₆
1. Hex to Bin: 1(0001) D(1101) B(1011)
2. Regroup by 3: (000) (111) (011) (011)
3. Bin to Octal:  0     7     3     3
Result: 733₈
